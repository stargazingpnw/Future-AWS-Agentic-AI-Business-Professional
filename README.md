# Future-AWS-Agentic-AI-Business-Professional-Portfolio
Welcome to my portfolio repository documenting my work in the Future AWS Agentic AI Business Professional Nanodegree track, fully sponsored by the AWS AI & ML Scholars Program in partnership with Udacity. This track emphasizes the business application, strategic ROI, governance, and architectural planning of Agentic AI systems. It bridges the gap between technical orchestration and real-world business intelligence.

📂 Portfolio Structure & Projects
This repository is split into modules following the core Udacity curriculum:

📊 Course 1: Business Intelligence & Knowledge Management
amazon-quicksight-dashboards/
Description: End-to-end interactive data dashboards built to resolve business operational blockers.
Tools: Amazon QuickSight, QuickSight Q (Natural Language Querying), and datasets.
Key Focus: Transforming raw operational data into actionable data assets, building functional KPI indicators, and deploying executive-level intelligence reports.

👔 Course 2: Agentic AI for Business Leaders
agentic-expense-reporting-system/
Description: Strategic analysis, debugging, and structured enhancement maps for a multi-agent corporate expense reporting system.
Key Focus: Analyzing workflows featuring multiple autonomous agents, integrating human-in-the-loop (HITL) review gates, and identifying critical performance guardrails.
Deliverables: Technical feasibility audits, Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool usage maps, and risk governance scorecards for executive alignment.

📈 Key Competencies Demonstrated
No-Code & Low-Code Workflow Design: Prototyping autonomous systems that act, reason, and collaborate without heavy engineering overhead.
Strategic AI Governance & ROI: Evaluating inference costs, scalability metrics, safety parameters, and token management strategies.
Data & Knowledge Integration: Leveraging cloud infrastructure to power AI solutions with clean corporate data while implementing strict guardrails.
